# Hotel Management System - i18n Terminology Glossary

This glossary standardizes key hotel management terminology used across the application in English and French. All translations must adhere to these standard terms.

| English Term | French Term | Context / Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Front Desk** | **Réception** | Front office counter, guest registration, check-in roster. |
| **Housekeeping** | **Gouvernance** | Housekeeping and room turnover team and department. |
| **Night Audit** | **Clôture journalière** | End-of-day accounting closure, business date transition, and posting of nightly room charges. |
| **Folio** | **Folio** | Guest master ledger, itemized stay invoice, debit/credit accounting. |
| **Check-in** | **Arrivée (Check-in)** | Physical guest arrival, key allocation, and folio activation. |
| **Check-out** | **Départ (Check-out)** | Guest departure, final folio settlement, key recovery, and status turnover to dirty. |
| **No-show** | **Non-présentation (No-show)** | A reserved guest who did not arrive on their scheduled arrival date. |
| **Deposit** | **Acompte (Arrhes)** | Advance payment or guarantee received before or at check-in. |
| **Walk-in** | **Client de passage (Walk-in)** | Guest arriving directly without an advance booking. |
| **Room Matrix** | **Plan des chambres** | Visual grid/matrix showing real-time room statuses and assignments. |
